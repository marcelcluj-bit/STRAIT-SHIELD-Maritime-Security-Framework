# STRAIT-SHIELD: Strait of Hormuz Maritime Security Framework

**Geostrategic Maritime Intelligence & Anomaly Detection System**  
*Framework: Real-time Multi-Sensor Satellite Data Ingestion powered by Gemini AI*

---

## 🌊 Vision & Scope
**STRAIT-SHIELD** is an advanced maritime situational awareness platform engineered to secure critical energy supply routes in the Strait of Hormuz. By fusing AIS transponder data, High-Resolution SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) satellite imagery, and naval thermal telemetry, the framework automatically identifies spoofed transponders, dark vessels, and potential asymmetric threats.

---

## 🛡️ Key Features
- **AIS vs. SAR Cross-Verification:** Detects hidden or unflagged vessels by matching physical radar signatures with transponder broadcasts.
- **Predictive Route Analysis:** Anticipates illegal boarding attempts, naval blockades, or unauthorized maritime diversions.
- **Dual-Use Compliance:** Designed to interface seamlessly with international maritime safety networks and sovereign coast guards.
